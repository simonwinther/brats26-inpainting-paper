\section{Results}

\subsection{Development Selection}
\label{sec:results:selection}

Figure~\ref{fig:checkpoint-selection} shows the periodic five-case development
diagnostics and selected EMA checkpoints. Fixed-mask steps 170,000 and 180,000 tie
for the lowest joint rank; step 180,000 is retained because it has the best
aggregate PSNR and MSE (and third-best SSIM). Random step 290,000 and
weighted-mixture step 200,000 are best on all three aggregate metrics within their
respective recorded histories.

  \caption{Raw five-case EMA checkpoint diagnostics without dashboard smoothing.
  Stars mark the retained checkpoint after ranking aggregate SSIM, PSNR, and MSE;
  fixed-mask 170k and 180k tie in average rank, with 180k retained because it leads
  PSNR and MSE. Each point uses one deterministic 1000-step trajectory. Curves are
  selection diagnostics, not confirmation results.}
  \label{fig:checkpoint-selection}
\end{figure}

Table~\ref{tab:ensemble-selection} summarizes inference-policy selection at the
terminal EMA checkpoints on the 25-case development set, including the five
diagnostic cases. Mean $N=5$ has the lowest joint rank for random and
weighted-mixture augmentation. The fixed-mask variant's nominal optimum is mean
$N=3$, but its rank advantage over $N=1$ is 0.1333 for three times as many reverse
trajectories; mean $N=5$ is additionally worse. We therefore select fixed mask
$N=1$ and random/weighted-mixture mean $N=5$.

\subsection{Held-out 75-Case Confirmation}
\label{sec:results:confirmation}

Table~\ref{tab:confirmation-results} summarizes the held-out confirmation results.
\input{confirmation75}

The omnibus difference is significant for SSIM
($\chi_F^2(2)=52.83$), PSNR, and MSE (both $\chi_F^2(2)=52.91$; all
$p<3.4\times10^{-12}$). In the non-compute-matched comparison with fixed mask,
weighted mixture improves mean SSIM by
$0.0411$ (95\% CI: $0.0324$ to $0.0500$), PSNR by $1.34$\,dB
($1.08$ to $1.61$), and reduces raw $[0,1]$-scale MSE by $0.003771$
($0.002882$ to $0.004746$).

Relative to the compute-matched random-augmentation pipeline, weighted mixture
improves SSIM by
$0.0188$ ($0.0127$ to $0.0249$), PSNR by $0.948$\,dB
($0.656$ to $1.265$), and reduces raw $[0,1]$-scale MSE by $0.002641$
($0.001878$ to $0.003447$). All nine metric-specific pairwise tests remain
significant after Holm correction within each metric family (largest adjusted
$p=0.0178$).

\begin{figure}[!t]
  \centering

  \caption{Compute-matched paired effects of weighted-mixture versus random

Figure~\ref{fig:confirmation-effects} shows that the compute-matched gain is not
universal but occurs in most cases: weighted mixture wins 54/75 cases on SSIM and
57/75 on both PSNR and MSE. It improves all three metrics against random
augmentation in 51 cases, loses all three in 15, and has mixed directions in 9.
Among all three selected pipelines, weighted mixture is simultaneously best on all
three metrics in 46 cases and obtains the lowest mean joint rank.

Figure~\ref{fig:qualitative-reconstructions} illustrates substantial variation in
case difficulty. The low-SSIM example retains gross structure but has a localized
intensity and boundary mismatch; the median is coherent with residual error in the
scored region; and the high-SSIM example closely matches the target there. Because
the observed reference may be pathological elsewhere in the hole, its score is not
evidence of counterfactual accuracy over the full cyan outline.
